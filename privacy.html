Privacy Policy

Caption Camera

Support Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Caption Camera is a camera app that stamps photos with the date, time, location, weather and compass heading, and helps you organize them into folders and reports. This policy explains what data the app uses and how. In short: your photos and everything about them stay on your iPhone. Caption Camera has no servers and no accounts, and collects nothing about you.

Data Caption Camera Uses

Location. When you allow it, Caption Camera reads your device's location to record coordinates, altitude and compass heading with each photo you take. This is stored with the photo, on your device.

Address lookup. To show an address or city on a photo, the app sends that photo's coordinates to Apple's location services to look up the place name.

Weather. To show the weather on a photo, the app sends the photo's coordinates (and, for a photo taken offline, its date and time) to Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Nothing else is sent — no name, account, device identifier or photo.

Maps. The Map tab, map-style stamps and the map on a PDF report's cover use Apple Maps, which receives the area being shown so it can return map images.

Camera. Used to take photos. Photos are saved inside the app.

Microphone. Used only while you record a voice note for a photo. Recordings are saved inside the app, beside the photo.

Photos library. The app can save a photo to your Photos library when you choose to. When you use Import from Photos, it receives only the photos you pick in the system picker, with their date and location; it cannot see the rest of your library.

Face ID. If you lock the Hidden album, iOS checks your Face ID or passcode. The app is only told whether it succeeded; it never receives your face data.

Data Storage

All photos, notes, voice notes, folders, presets and settings are stored only on your device. Caption Camera does not upload, sync or back up your photos to any server. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Data You Choose to Share

Photos, PDF reports, ZIP exports and library backups leave your device only when you share or save them yourself — for example by AirDrop, Messages, Mail or the Files app. Exports include the details recorded with each photo (date, location, weather, heading and notes), so check them before sending.

Data Sharing

Caption Camera does not sell or share your data with anyone, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Tracking

Caption Camera does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Children

Caption Camera does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

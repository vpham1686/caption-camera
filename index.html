Common questions

Why does the app ask for my location?
So it can stamp GPS coordinates, the city and the address onto your photos, and place them on the map. If you decline, everything else still works — you just get the date and time.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?
No. Photos stay on your device. There is no account, no server and no sync.

Can I change what appears on the stamp?
Yes. Tap the sliders icon on the camera screen to choose which fields show, their order, position, size, colour and opacity. Save combinations you like as presets.

Can I edit a photo after taking it?
Yes. Open a photo and tap Edit to crop, straighten, draw on it, change its filter, or change the stamp. Edits always use the date and location recorded when the photo was taken, never your current location.

I deleted a photo by mistake.
Check Recently Deleted under Collections on the Folders tab. Deleted photos stay there for 30 days by default, adjustable in Settings. Right after deleting, you can also tap Undo.

How do I back up my photos?
Photos live only on your iPhone. In Settings, tap Export Library Backup to save a copy of everything to Files or iCloud Drive. To send a set of photos to someone, use Share as ZIP or Create PDF Report from a folder's ••• menu.
